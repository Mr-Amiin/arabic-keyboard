Yusuf’s trip

Level 3: Beginner stories

Read the story, then answer the questions. All texts are Modern Standard Arabic.

  1. يُوسُف طَالِب مِنْ مِصْر.
    yūsuf ṭālib min miṣr.
    Yusuf is a student from Egypt.
  2. هُوَ يُسَافِرُ إِلَى الْأُرْدُنّ بِالطَّائِرَة.
    huwa yusāfiru ilā l-urdunn bi-ṭ-ṭāʾira.
    He travels to Jordan by plane.
  3. يَصِلُ إِلَى عَمَّان فِي الْمَسَاء.
    yaṣilu ilā ʿammān fī l-masāʾ.
    He arrives in Amman in the evening.
  4. الطَّقْس بَارِد فِي عَمَّان.
    aṭ-ṭaqs bārid fī ʿammān.
    The weather is cold in Amman.
  5. يَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْفُنْدُق بِسَيَّارَة أُجْرَة.
    yadhhabu ilā l-funduq bi-sayyārat ujra.
    He goes to the hotel by taxi.
  6. يُحِبُّ يُوسُف الْأُرْدُنّ.
    yuḥibbu yūsuf al-urdunn.
    Yusuf likes Jordan.

Vocabulary explained

  • عَمَّان
    ʿammān — Amman (the capital of Jordan)
  • بِـ
    bi- — by, with (joined to the next word): بِالطَّائِرَة “by plane”
  • يَصِلُ
    yaṣilu — he arrives
