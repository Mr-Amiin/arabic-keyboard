Days, months and time

Day names begin with الـ (“the”). The months here are the Gregorian names used in Egypt, the Gulf and North Africa. In Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Palestine and Iraq a different set is used: see the regional variants below. These month names are borrowed words, so their vowelling follows common convention.

  • الْأَحَد
    al-aḥad — Sunday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling الأحد
    From أَحَد “one”: the first day of the week in the traditional count.
  • الِاثْنَيْن
    al-ithnayn — Monday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling الاثنين
    From “two”. The first letter after الـ is a connecting alif (hamzat wasl).
  • الثُّلَاثَاء
    ath-thulāthāʾ — Tuesday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling الثلاثاء
    From “three”.
  • الْأَرْبِعَاء
    al-arbiʿāʾ — Wednesday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling الأربعاء
    From “four”. Also pronounced al-arbaʿāʾ.
  • الْخَمِيس
    al-khamīs — Thursday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling الخميس
    From “five”.
  • الْجُمُعَة
    al-jumuʿa — Friday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling الجمعة
    The day of the weekly congregational prayer.
  • السَّبْت
    as-sabt — Saturday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling السبت
  • يَنَايِر
    yanāyir — January
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يناير
    Gregorian month name used in Egypt, the Gulf and North Africa.
  • فِبْرَايِر
    fibrāyir — February
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling فبراير
  • مَارِس
    māris — March
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling مارس
  • أَبْرِيل
    abrīl — April
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling أبريل
  • مَايُو
    māyū — May
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling مايو
  • يُونْيُو
    yūnyū — June
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يونيو
  • يُولْيُو
    yūlyū — July
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يوليو
  • أُغُسْطُس
    ughusṭus — August
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling أغسطس
  • سِبْتَمْبَر
    sibtambar — September
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling سبتمبر
  • أُكْتُوبَر
    uktūbar — October
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling أكتوبر
  • نُوفَمْبَر
    nūfambar — November
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling نوفمبر
  • دِيسَمْبَر
    dīsambar — December
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling ديسمبر
  • يَوْم
    yawm — day
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يوم
  • أُسْبُوع
    usbūʿ — week
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling أسبوع
  • شَهْر
    shahr — month
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling شهر
  • سَنَة
    sana — year
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling سنة
  • سَاعَة
    sāʿa — hour; clock, watch
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling ساعة
    One word for three meanings: an hour, a clock and a watch.
  • دَقِيقَة
    daqīqa — minute
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling دقيقة
  • الْيَوْم
    al-yawm — today
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling اليوم
    Literally “the day”.
  • غَدًا
    ghadan — tomorrow
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling غدا
    The -an ending (tanween fatha) is part of the standard form of this adverb.
  • أَمْس
    ams — yesterday
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling أمس
  • الْآن
    al-āna — now
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling الآن
    Written with the madda sign آ (alif with a long mark).
  • صَبَاح
    ṣabāḥ — morning
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling صباح
    فِي الصَّبَاح “in the morning”.
  • مَسَاء
    masāʾ — evening
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مساء
    فِي الْمَسَاء “in the evening”.
  • لَيْل
    layl — night
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling ليل
    فِي اللَّيْل “at night”.

Regional words and usage (not quizzed)

These are shown so you can recognise them. Each is labelled with its region and whether it is spoken dialect or standard Arabic used in only some countries.

  • دِلْوَقْتِي
    dilwaʾti — now
    Egyptian Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses الْآن.
  • هَلَّأ
    hallaʾ — now
    Levantine Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses الْآن. Spelling varies (هلأ, هلق).
  • هَالْحِين
    hālḥīn — now
    Gulf Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses الْآن.
  • بُكْرَة
    bukra — tomorrow
    Egyptian, Levantine and Gulf Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses غَدًا.
  • كَانُون الثَّانِي
    kānūn ath-thānī — January
    Levant and Iraq · standard Arabic, regional usage
    Standard month names used in Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Palestine and Iraq.
  • شُبَاط
    shubāṭ — February
    Levant and Iraq · standard Arabic, regional usage
    Standard month names used in Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Palestine and Iraq.
