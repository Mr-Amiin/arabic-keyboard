Places and directions

Direction words are often used with عَلَى (“on”): عَلَى الْيَمِين “on the right”. اِذْهَبْ is a command addressed to a man; to a woman say اِذْهَبِي.

  • مَدِينَة
    madīna — city, town
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مدينة
  • قَرْيَة
    qarya — village
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling قرية
  • شَارِع
    shāriʿ — street
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling شارع
  • سُوق
    sūq — market
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling سوق
  • مَدْرَسَة
    madrasa — school
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مدرسة
  • مَسْجِد
    masjid — mosque
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مسجد
  • مُسْتَشْفَى
    mustashfā — hospital
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مستشفى
    Written with ى (alif maqsura) at the end, but read as long ā.
  • مَطْعَم
    maṭʿam — restaurant
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مطعم
  • بَنْك
    bank — bank
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling بنك
  • مَطَار
    maṭār — airport
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مطار
  • مَحَطَّة
    maḥaṭṭa — station, stop
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling محطة
  • صَيْدَلِيَّة
    ṣaydaliyya — pharmacy
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling صيدلية
  • مَكْتَبَة
    maktaba — library, bookshop
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مكتبة
  • يَمِين
    yamīn — right (direction)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling يمين
    عَلَى الْيَمِين “on the right”.
  • يَسَار
    yasār — left (direction)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling يسار
    عَلَى الْيَسَار “on the left”.
  • أَمَام
    amām — in front of
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أمام
  • خَلْف
    khalf — behind
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling خلف
  • بِجَانِب
    bi-jānib — next to, beside
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling بجانب
    Written as one word: بِـ (“at”) + جَانِب (“side”).
  • قَرِيب
    qarīb — near, close
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling قريب
    Feminine: قَرِيبَة (qarība).
  • بَعِيد
    baʿīd — far
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling بعيد
    Feminine: بَعِيدَة (baʿīda).
  • مُسْتَقِيم
    mustaqīm — straight ahead
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مستقيم
    اِذْهَبْ مُسْتَقِيمًا “go straight”.
  • هُنَا
    hunā — here
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling هنا
  • هُنَاك
    hunāk — there
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling هناك
    هَلْ هُنَاك … ؟ means “Is there …?”
  • اِذْهَبْ
    idhhab — go! (to a man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling اذهب
    A command. To a woman: اِذْهَبِي (idhhabī). The first letter is a connecting alif (hamzat wasl).

Regional words and usage (not quizzed)

These are shown so you can recognise them. Each is labelled with its region and whether it is spoken dialect or standard Arabic used in only some countries.

  • دُغْرِي
    dughrī — straight ahead
    Egyptian and Levantine Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses مُسْتَقِيم.
