School and work

Many job words have a feminine form ending in ـَة (طَبِيب → طَبِيبَة), and the male form is used when the speaker or the group is unspecified or mixed.

  • جَامِعَة
    jāmiʿa — university
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling جامعة
  • طَالِب
    ṭālib — student (male)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling طالب
    Female: طَالِبَة.
  • طَالِبَة
    ṭāliba — student (female)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling طالبة
  • مُعَلِّم
    muʿallim — teacher (male)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling معلم
    Female: مُعَلِّمَة.
  • مُعَلِّمَة
    muʿallima — teacher (female)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling معلمة
  • كِتَاب
    kitāb — book
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling كتاب
  • قَلَم
    qalam — pen
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling قلم
  • دَفْتَر
    daftar — notebook
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling دفتر
  • مَكْتَب
    maktab — office, desk
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مكتب
  • دَرْس
    dars — lesson
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling درس
  • فَصْل
    faṣl — classroom, class
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling فصل
    The word also means “chapter” and “season”.
  • سُؤَال
    suʾāl — question
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling سؤال
  • جَوَاب
    jawāb — answer
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling جواب
  • اِمْتِحَان
    imtiḥān — exam
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling امتحان
    The first letter is a connecting alif (hamzat wasl).
  • شَرِكَة
    sharika — company
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling شركة
  • عَمَل
    ʿamal — work, job
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling عمل
  • وَظِيفَة
    waẓīfa — job, position
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling وظيفة
  • طَبِيب
    ṭabīb — doctor (male)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling طبيب
    Female: طَبِيبَة.
  • مُهَنْدِس
    muhandis — engineer (male)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مهندس
    Female: مُهَنْدِسَة.
  • مُدِير
    mudīr — manager, director (male)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مدير
    Female: مُدِيرَة.
