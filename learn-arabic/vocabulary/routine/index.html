Daily routines and common verbs

Each verb is the present-tense “he” form (يَذْهَبُ = “he goes”); the past tense is given in the note. To say “I” the first letter changes to أ (أَذْهَبُ = “I go”). The final ـُ is the ending of the present tense; it is usually dropped when a word is spoken at the end of a sentence.

  • يَذْهَبُ
    yadhhabu — to go (he goes)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يذهب
    Past: ذَهَبَ (dhahaba). I go: أَذْهَبُ.
  • يَأْكُلُ
    yaʾkulu — to eat (he eats)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يأكل
    Past: أَكَلَ (akala). I eat: آكُلُ (ākulu).
  • يَشْرَبُ
    yashrabu — to drink (he drinks)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يشرب
    Past: شَرِبَ (shariba). I drink: أَشْرَبُ.
  • يَنَامُ
    yanāmu — to sleep (he sleeps)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling ينام
    Past: نَامَ (nāma). I sleep: أَنَامُ.
  • يَسْتَيْقِظُ
    yastayqiẓu — to wake up (he wakes up)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يستيقظ
    Past: اِسْتَيْقَظَ (istayqaẓa). I wake up: أَسْتَيْقِظُ.
  • يَقْرَأُ
    yaqraʾu — to read (he reads)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يقرأ
    Past: قَرَأَ (qaraʾa). I read: أَقْرَأُ.
  • يَكْتُبُ
    yaktubu — to write (he writes)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يكتب
    Past: كَتَبَ (kataba). I write: أَكْتُبُ.
  • يَعْمَلُ
    yaʿmalu — to work (he works)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يعمل
    Past: عَمِلَ (ʿamila). I work: أَعْمَلُ.
  • يَدْرُسُ
    yadrusu — to study (he studies)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يدرس
    Past: دَرَسَ (darasa). I study: أَدْرُسُ.
  • يَجْلِسُ
    yajlisu — to sit (he sits)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يجلس
    Past: جَلَسَ (jalasa). I sit: أَجْلِسُ.
  • يَسْكُنُ
    yaskunu — to live, reside (he lives)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يسكن
    Past: سَكَنَ (sakana). I live: أَسْكُنُ.
  • يُحِبُّ
    yuḥibbu — to love, to like (he likes)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يحب
    Past: أَحَبَّ (aḥabba). I like: أُحِبُّ.
  • يُرِيدُ
    yurīdu — to want (he wants)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يريد
    Past: أَرَادَ (arāda). I want: أُرِيدُ. The noun that follows takes -an: أُرِيدُ شَايًا “I want tea”.
  • يَتَكَلَّمُ
    yatakallamu — to speak (he speaks)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يتكلم
    Past: تَكَلَّمَ (takallama). I speak: أَتَكَلَّمُ.
  • يَفْهَمُ
    yafhamu — to understand (he understands)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يفهم
    Past: فَهِمَ (fahima). I understand: أَفْهَمُ.
  • يَرْجِعُ
    yarjiʿu — to return, to go back (he returns)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يرجع
    Past: رَجَعَ (rajaʿa). I return: أَرْجِعُ.
  • يَمْشِي
    yamshī — to walk (he walks)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يمشي
    Past: مَشَى (mashā). I walk: أَمْشِي.
  • يَفْعَلُ
    yafʿalu — to do (he does)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يفعل
    Past: فَعَلَ (faʿala). مَاذَا تَفْعَلُ؟ “What do you do?”
  • يَقُولُ
    yaqūlu — to say (he says)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يقول
    Past: قَالَ (qāla). I say: أَقُولُ.
  • يَسْأَلُ
    yasʾalu — to ask (he asks)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يسأل
    Past: سَأَلَ (saʾala). I ask: أَسْأَلُ.
  • يَغْسِلُ
    yaghsilu — to wash (he washes)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يغسل
    Past: غَسَلَ (ghasala). I wash: أَغْسِلُ.
  • يَلْعَبُ
    yalʿabu — to play (he plays)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يلعب
    Past: لَعِبَ (laʿiba). I play: أَلْعَبُ.
  • يَسْمَعُ
    yasmaʿu — to hear, to listen (he hears)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يسمع
    Past: سَمِعَ (samiʿa). I hear: أَسْمَعُ.

Regional words and usage (not quizzed)

These are shown so you can recognise them. Each is labelled with its region and whether it is spoken dialect or standard Arabic used in only some countries.

  • عَايِز
    ʿāyiz — I want (said by a man)
    Egyptian Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses أُرِيدُ.
  • بِدِّي
    biddī — I want
    Levantine Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses أُرِيدُ.
  • أَبِي
    abī — I want
    Gulf Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses أُرِيدُ.
