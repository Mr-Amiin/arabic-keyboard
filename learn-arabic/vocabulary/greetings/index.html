Greetings and everyday expressions

Some expressions change for a woman (for example كَيْفَ حَالُكِ؟). The forms here are for speaking to a man.

  • مَرْحَبًا
    marḥaban — hello
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مرحبا
  • أَهْلًا
    ahlan — hi, welcome
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أهلا
    Often extended to أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا (welcome).
  • السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ
    as-salāmu ʿalaykum — peace be upon you (a greeting)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling السلام عليكم
    A greeting used across the Arabic-speaking world.
  • وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ
    wa-ʿalaykumu s-salām — and upon you be peace (the reply)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling وعليكم السلام
    The traditional reply to السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ.
  • صَبَاحُ الْخَيْرِ
    ṣabāḥu l-khayr — good morning
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling صباح الخير
  • صَبَاحُ النُّورِ
    ṣabāḥu n-nūr — good morning (the reply; literally “morning of light”)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling صباح النور
  • مَسَاءُ الْخَيْرِ
    masāʾu l-khayr — good evening
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مساء الخير
  • مَعَ السَّلَامَةِ
    maʿa s-salāma — goodbye (literally “with safety”)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مع السلامة
  • شُكْرًا
    shukran — thank you
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling شكرا
  • عَفْوًا
    ʿafwan — you’re welcome (also “excuse me”)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling عفوا
  • مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
    min faḍlika — please (to a man)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling من فضلك
    To a woman: مِنْ فَضْلِكِ.
  • نَعَمْ
    naʿam — yes
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling نعم
  • لَا
    lā — no
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling لا
  • كَيْفَ حَالُكَ؟
    kayfa ḥāluka — how are you? (to a man)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling كيف حالك
    To a woman: كَيْفَ حَالُكِ؟
  • بِخَيْرٍ
    bi-khayrin — fine, well (a reply to “how are you?”)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling بخير
  • الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ
    al-ḥamdu lillāhi — praise be to God (also a common answer to “how are you?”)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling الحمد لله
  • مَا اسْمُكَ؟
    mā smuka — what is your name? (to a man)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling ما اسمك
    To a woman: مَا اسْمُكِ؟
  • اِسْمِي أَحْمَد
    ismī aḥmad — my name is Ahmad
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling اسمي أحمد
    Replace أَحْمَد with your own name.

Regional variants (not Modern Standard Arabic)

These are shown so you can recognise them; they are not quizzed.

  • إِزَّيَّك؟
    izzayyak — how are you? (to a man)
    Egyptian Arabic · dialect
  • كِيفَك؟
    kīfak — how are you? (to a man)
    Levantine Arabic (e.g. Lebanon, Syria, Jordan, Palestine) · dialect
  • شْلُونَك؟
    shlōnak — how are you? (to a man)
    Gulf and Iraqi Arabic · dialect
