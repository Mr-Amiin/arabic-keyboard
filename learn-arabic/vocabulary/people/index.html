People, countries and nationalities

Nationality words agree with the speaker: the forms here are masculine; add ـَة for a woman (مِصْرِيّ → مِصْرِيَّة). After مِنْ (“from”) a country name is used: أَنَا مِنْ مِصْر.

  • أَنَا
    anā — I
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أنا
    Used for both men and women.
  • أَنْتَ
    anta — you (to a man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أنت
  • أَنْتِ
    anti — you (to a woman)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أنت
    Only the last vowel differs from أَنْتَ.
  • هُوَ
    huwa — he
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling هو
  • هِيَ
    hiya — she
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling هي
  • نَحْنُ
    naḥnu — we
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling نحن
  • اِسْم
    ism — name
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling اسم
    The first letter is a connecting alif (hamzat wasl). “My name”: اِسْمِي.
  • جِنْسِيَّة
    jinsiyya — nationality
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling جنسية
  • مِنْ
    min — from
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling من
    Without harakat it is written exactly like مَنْ (“who”): only the vowel mark tells them apart.
  • مِصْر
    miṣr — Egypt
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مصر
  • سُورِيَا
    sūriyā — Syria
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling سوريا
    Also spelled سُورِيَّة (sūriyya).
  • السُّعُودِيَّة
    as-suʿūdiyya — Saudi Arabia
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling السعودية
    The name begins with الـ (“the”).
  • الْأُرْدُنّ
    al-urdunn — Jordan
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling الأردن
    The name begins with الـ (“the”).
  • لُبْنَان
    lubnān — Lebanon
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling لبنان
  • الْمَغْرِب
    al-maghrib — Morocco
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling المغرب
    The name begins with الـ (“the”). The word also means “the west”.
  • أَمْرِيكَا
    amrīkā — America (the United States)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أمريكا
  • مِصْرِيّ
    miṣriyy — Egyptian (man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مصري
    Woman: مِصْرِيَّة (miṣriyya).
  • سُورِيّ
    sūriyy — Syrian (man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling سوري
    Woman: سُورِيَّة (sūriyya).
  • سُعُودِيّ
    suʿūdiyy — Saudi (man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling سعودي
    Woman: سُعُودِيَّة (suʿūdiyya).
  • أُرْدُنِّيّ
    urdunniyy — Jordanian (man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling أردني
    Woman: أُرْدُنِّيَّة (urdunniyya).
  • لُبْنَانِيّ
    lubnāniyy — Lebanese (man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling لبناني
    Woman: لُبْنَانِيَّة (lubnāniyya).
  • مَغْرِبِيّ
    maghribiyy — Moroccan (man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مغربي
    Woman: مَغْرِبِيَّة (maghribiyya).
  • أَمْرِيكِيّ
    amrīkiyy — American (man)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling أمريكي
    Woman: أَمْرِيكِيَّة (amrīkiyya).
  • تَشَرَّفْنَا
    tasharrafnā — nice to meet you
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling تشرفنا
    Literally “we are honoured”. Said by both people when they meet.
  • يَا
    yā — O … (used before a name)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling يا
    A particle placed before the name of the person you speak to: يَا أَحْمَد.
