Food and drinks

Several food words are collective nouns (تُفَّاح “apples”, سَمَك “fish”, بَيْض “eggs”): add ـَة for a single piece (تُفَّاحَة “one apple”).

  • مَاء
    māʾ — water
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling ماء
    The final ء is a hamza written on the line.
  • حَلِيب
    ḥalīb — milk
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling حليب
  • شَاي
    shāy — tea
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling شاي
  • قَهْوَة
    qahwa — coffee
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling قهوة
    The word also means a café in some regions.
  • عَصِير
    ʿaṣīr — juice
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling عصير
  • خُبْز
    khubz — bread
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling خبز
  • جُبْن
    jubn — cheese
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling جبن
  • بَيْض
    bayḍ — eggs
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling بيض
    A collective noun. One egg: بَيْضَة (bayḍa).
  • لَحْم
    laḥm — meat
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling لحم
  • دَجَاج
    dajāj — chicken
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling دجاج
    A collective noun, used for the birds and the meat.
  • سَمَك
    samak — fish
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling سمك
    A collective noun. One fish: سَمَكَة (samaka).
  • تُفَّاح
    tuffāḥ — apples
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling تفاح
    A collective noun. One apple: تُفَّاحَة (tuffāḥa).
  • مَوْز
    mawz — bananas
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling موز
    A collective noun. One banana: مَوْزَة (mawza).
  • مِلْح
    milḥ — salt
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling ملح
  • سُكَّر
    sukkar — sugar
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling سكر
  • طَعَام
    ṭaʿām — food
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling طعام
  • فَطُور
    faṭūr — breakfast
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling فطور
  • غَدَاء
    ghadāʾ — lunch
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling غداء
  • عَشَاء
    ʿashāʾ — dinner
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling عشاء

Regional words and usage (not quizzed)

These are shown so you can recognise them. Each is labelled with its region and whether it is spoken dialect or standard Arabic used in only some countries.

  • عَيْش
    ʿaysh — bread
    Egyptian Arabic · dialect
    In Egypt عَيْش is the everyday word for bread. In Modern Standard Arabic عَيْش means “living”.
