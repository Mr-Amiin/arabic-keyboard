Question words and small words

These small words are the glue of every sentence. هَلْ turns a statement into a yes/no question. Adjectives here are masculine singular; most add ـَة for a feminine noun (كَبِير → كَبِيرَة).

  • مَنْ
    man — who
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling من
    Without harakat it is written exactly like مِنْ (“from”): only the vowel mark tells them apart.
  • مَا
    mā — what (before a noun)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling ما
    Used before a noun: مَا اسْمُكَ؟ “What is your name?”
  • مَاذَا
    mādhā — what (before a verb)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling ماذا
    Used before a verb: مَاذَا تُرِيدُ؟ “What do you want?”
  • أَيْنَ
    ayna — where
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أين
  • مَتَى
    matā — when
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling متى
  • كَيْفَ
    kayfa — how
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling كيف
  • لِمَاذَا
    li-mādhā — why
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling لماذا
  • هَلْ
    hal — (question word for yes/no questions)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling هل
    Put it at the start of a statement to ask a yes/no question: هَلْ تَعْمَلُ؟ “Do you work?”
  • فِي
    fī — in, at
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling في
  • إِلَى
    ilā — to, towards
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling إلى
    Written with ى (alif maqsura) at the end, but read as long ā.
  • عَلَى
    ʿalā — on
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling على
    Written with ى (alif maqsura) at the end, but read as long ā.
  • عِنْدَ
    ʿinda — at, with (used to say “have”)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling عند
    عِنْدِي = “I have”, عِنْدَكَ = “you (m) have”, عِنْدَكِ = “you (f) have”.
  • حَسَنًا
    ḥasanan — OK, very well, fine
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling حسنا
    Said to agree or accept something. The -an ending (tanween fatha) is part of the standard form.
  • ثُمَّ
    thumma — then
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling ثم
  • كُلّ
    kull — every, all
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling كل
    Followed by a singular noun: كُلَّ يَوْم “every day”.
  • كَبِير
    kabīr — big, old (of a person: elder)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling كبير
  • صَغِير
    ṣaghīr — small, young
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling صغير
  • جَدِيد
    jadīd — new
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling جديد
  • جَيِّد
    jayyid — good
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling جيد
  • جَمِيل
    jamīl — beautiful, nice
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling جميل
  • مَرِيض
    marīḍ — sick, ill
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مريض
  • جَائِع
    jāʾiʿ — hungry
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling جائع
  • سَعِيد
    saʿīd — happy
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling سعيد
  • مُتْعَب
    mutʿab — tired
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling متعب
