Shopping and money

Currency names differ by country: دِينَار (for example Jordan, Iraq and Kuwait), رِيَال (for example Saudi Arabia and Qatar), دِرْهَم (for example the UAE and Morocco). With the numbers 3–10 the counted noun is plural: عَشَرَة دَنَانِير “ten dinars”.

  • مَتْجَر
    matjar — shop, store
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling متجر
  • مَال
    māl — money, wealth
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مال
  • نُقُود
    nuqūd — money, cash
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling نقود
    A plural form used as the everyday word for money.
  • سِعْر
    siʿr — price
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling سعر
  • رَخِيص
    rakhīṣ — cheap
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling رخيص
  • غَالٍ
    ghālin — expensive
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling غال
    In the indefinite form the final ي is dropped and a tanween kasra is written: غَالٍ.
  • كَمْ
    kam — how much, how many
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling كم
  • بِكَمْ
    bikam — for how much? (asking a price)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling بكم
    بِـ (“for”) + كَمْ.
  • بِكَمْ هَٰذَا؟
    bikam hādhā — how much is this?
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling بكم هذا
    To ask about a feminine thing: بِكَمْ هَٰذِهِ؟
  • يَشْتَرِي
    yashtarī — to buy (he buys)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يشتري
    Past: اِشْتَرَى (ishtarā). I buy: أَشْتَرِي.
  • يَبِيعُ
    yabīʿu — to sell (he sells)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يبيع
    Past: بَاعَ (bāʿa). I sell: أَبِيعُ.
  • يَدْفَعُ
    yadfaʿu — to pay (he pays)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يدفع
    Past: دَفَعَ (dafaʿa). I pay: أَدْفَعُ.
  • دِينَار
    dīnār — dinar
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling دينار
    Plural: دَنَانِير (danānīr).
  • رِيَال
    riyāl — riyal
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling ريال
  • دِرْهَم
    dirham — dirham
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling درهم
  • دُولَار
    dūlār — dollar
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling دولار
  • فَاتُورَة
    fātūra — bill, invoice
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling فاتورة
  • تَخْفِيض
    takhfīḍ — discount, reduction
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling تخفيض
  • مَقَاس
    maqās — size (of clothes, shoes)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مقاس
  • تَفَضَّلْ
    tafaḍḍal — here you are; please go ahead (to a man)
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling تفضل
    To a woman: تَفَضَّلِي (tafaḍḍalī). Said when handing something over or inviting someone to enter or sit.

Regional words and usage (not quizzed)

These are shown so you can recognise them. Each is labelled with its region and whether it is spoken dialect or standard Arabic used in only some countries.

  • بِكَام
    bikām — how much?
    Egyptian Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses بِكَمْ.
  • قَدَّيْش
    ʾaddaysh — how much?
    Levantine Arabic · dialect
    Modern Standard Arabic uses بِكَمْ.
