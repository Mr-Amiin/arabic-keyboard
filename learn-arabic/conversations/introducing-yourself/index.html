Unit 1: Introducing yourself

Two people meet for the first time and introduce themselves.

Register: Modern Standard Arabic. The dialogue is Modern Standard Arabic. Spoken dialect forms appear only in the labelled notes and are not quizzed.

Dialogue

  1. Ahmad (a man)مَرْحَبًا! اِسْمِي أَحْمَد.
    marḥaban! ismī aḥmad.
    Hello! My name is Ahmad.
  2. Sara (a woman)أَهْلًا يَا أَحْمَد! أَنَا سَارَة.
    ahlan yā aḥmad! anā sāra.
    Hi, Ahmad! I am Sara.
  3. Ahmad (a man)أَنَا طَالِب. وَأَنْتِ؟
    anā ṭālib. wa-anti?
    I am a student. And you?
  4. Sara (a woman)أَنَا مُعَلِّمَة.
    anā muʿallima.
    I am a teacher.
  5. Ahmad (a man)تَشَرَّفْنَا يَا سَارَة.
    tasharrafnā yā sāra.
    Nice to meet you, Sara.
  6. Sara (a woman)تَشَرَّفْنَا.
    tasharrafnā.
    Nice to meet you.

Key vocabulary

  • مَرْحَبًا
    marḥaban — hello
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling مرحبا
  • أَهْلًا
    ahlan — hi, welcome
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أهلا
    Often extended to أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا (welcome).
  • اِسْمِي أَحْمَد
    ismī aḥmad — my name is Ahmad
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling اسمي أحمد
    Replace أَحْمَد with your own name.
  • أَنَا
    anā — I
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أنا
    Used for both men and women.
  • أَنْتِ
    anti — you (to a woman)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling أنت
    Only the last vowel differs from أَنْتَ.
  • يَا
    yā — O … (used before a name)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling يا
    A particle placed before the name of the person you speak to: يَا أَحْمَد.
  • تَشَرَّفْنَا
    tasharrafnā — nice to meet you
    phrase · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling تشرفنا
    Literally “we are honoured”. Said by both people when they meet.
  • طَالِب
    ṭālib — student (male)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling طالب
    Female: طَالِبَة.
  • مُعَلِّمَة
    muʿallima — teacher (female)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling معلمة

Spoken variants (not Modern Standard Arabic)

People often say it differently in speech. These are shown for recognition only and are not quizzed.

  • فُرْصَة سَعِيدَة
    furṣa saʿīda — nice to meet you (“a happy opportunity”)
    Egyptian Arabic (also heard elsewhere) · dialect
    Common in speech. تَشَرَّفْنَا is also widely understood.

Speaking practice

Listen to each line, then say it aloud two or three times.

  • مَرْحَبًا! اِسْمِي أَحْمَد.
    Line 1: مَرْحَبًا: the ح (ḥ) is a breathy sound made in the throat. It is not the English h.
  • أَهْلًا يَا أَحْمَد! أَنَا سَارَة.
    Line 2: سَارَة: the ā is a long vowel, held about twice as long as a short vowel. The ر is a quick tap of the tongue, not the English r.
  • تَشَرَّفْنَا يَا سَارَة.
    Line 5: تَشَرَّفْنَا: the shadda on ر means the sound is doubled; hold it briefly (tasharr-afnā).
