Unit 8: Talking about daily activities

Layla asks Yusuf about his day.

Register: Modern Standard Arabic. The dialogue is Modern Standard Arabic. Spoken dialect forms appear only in the labelled notes and are not quizzed.

Dialogue

  1. Layla (a woman)مَاذَا تَفْعَلُ كُلَّ يَوْم؟
    mādhā tafʿalu kulla yawm?
    What do you do every day?
  2. Yusuf (a man)أَسْتَيْقِظُ فِي الصَّبَاح ثُمَّ أَشْرَبُ الْقَهْوَة.
    astayqiẓu fī ṣ-ṣabāḥ thumma ashrabu l-qahwa.
    I wake up in the morning, then I drink the coffee.
  3. Layla (a woman)هَلْ تَعْمَلُ؟
    hal taʿmalu?
    Do you work?
  4. Yusuf (a man)نَعَمْ، أَعْمَلُ فِي شَرِكَة.
    naʿam, aʿmalu fī sharika.
    Yes, I work in a company.
  5. Layla (a woman)وَمَاذَا تَفْعَلُ فِي الْمَسَاء؟
    wa-mādhā tafʿalu fī l-masāʾ?
    And what do you do in the evening?
  6. Yusuf (a man)أَقْرَأُ كِتَابًا ثُمَّ أَنَام.
    aqraʾu kitāban thumma anām.
    I read a book, then I sleep.

Key vocabulary

  • مَاذَا
    mādhā — what (before a verb)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling ماذا
    Used before a verb: مَاذَا تُرِيدُ؟ “What do you want?”
  • يَفْعَلُ
    yafʿalu — to do (he does)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يفعل
    Past: فَعَلَ (faʿala). مَاذَا تَفْعَلُ؟ “What do you do?”
  • كُلّ
    kull — every, all
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling كل
    Followed by a singular noun: كُلَّ يَوْم “every day”.
  • يَوْم
    yawm — day
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يوم
  • يَسْتَيْقِظُ
    yastayqiẓu — to wake up (he wakes up)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Challenging · plain spelling يستيقظ
    Past: اِسْتَيْقَظَ (istayqaẓa). I wake up: أَسْتَيْقِظُ.
  • صَبَاح
    ṣabāḥ — morning
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling صباح
    فِي الصَّبَاح “in the morning”.
  • فِي
    fī — in, at
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling في
  • ثُمَّ
    thumma — then
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling ثم
  • يَشْرَبُ
    yashrabu — to drink (he drinks)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يشرب
    Past: شَرِبَ (shariba). I drink: أَشْرَبُ.
  • قَهْوَة
    qahwa — coffee
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling قهوة
    The word also means a café in some regions.
  • يَعْمَلُ
    yaʿmalu — to work (he works)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يعمل
    Past: عَمِلَ (ʿamila). I work: أَعْمَلُ.
  • شَرِكَة
    sharika — company
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling شركة
  • مَسَاء
    masāʾ — evening
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling مساء
    فِي الْمَسَاء “in the evening”.
  • يَقْرَأُ
    yaqraʾu — to read (he reads)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling يقرأ
    Past: قَرَأَ (qaraʾa). I read: أَقْرَأُ.
  • كِتَاب
    kitāb — book
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling كتاب
  • يَنَامُ
    yanāmu — to sleep (he sleeps)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Medium · plain spelling ينام
    Past: نَامَ (nāma). I sleep: أَنَامُ.
  • هَلْ
    hal — (question word for yes/no questions)
    word · Modern Standard Arabic · Easy · plain spelling هل
    Put it at the start of a statement to ask a yes/no question: هَلْ تَعْمَلُ؟ “Do you work?”

Spoken variants (not Modern Standard Arabic)

People often say it differently in speech. These are shown for recognition only and are not quizzed.

  • بَشْرَب
    bashrab — I drink / I am drinking
    Levantine Arabic · dialect
    Egyptian and Levantine speakers usually add a prefix b- to present-tense verbs. Modern Standard Arabic has no such prefix: أَشْرَبُ.

Speaking practice

Listen to each line, then say it aloud two or three times.

  • أَسْتَيْقِظُ فِي الصَّبَاح ثُمَّ أَشْرَبُ الْقَهْوَة.
    Line 2: أَسْتَيْقِظُ: the ق (q) is made deeper in the throat than the English k, and the ظ (ẓ) is an emphatic, heavy th.
  • مَاذَا تَفْعَلُ كُلَّ يَوْم؟
    Line 1: تَفْعَلُ: begin the second syllable with the deep ع (ʿayn).
  • أَقْرَأُ كِتَابًا ثُمَّ أَنَام.
    Line 6: أَقْرَأُ: the ق (q) again, deeper than k; the final ء is a short stop of the breath.
