Arabic letter Sin (س): sound, shapes and example words

Sin (سين) is letter 12 of the 28 Arabic letters. Sound: s, as in “sun”. It joins to the next letter, so it has four shapes.

Letter 12 of 28: Sin

س

سين · Sin

Easy

Sound: s, as in “sun”

How to say it

Same as English s.

Transliteration is only a rough guide. Audio uses your device’s text-to-speech voice where available; it approximates pronunciation but is not a native recording.

Letter shapes

سAlone
سـBeginning
ـسـMiddle
ـسEnd

س joins to the letter after it, so it has four shapes depending on where it sits in a word.

How to tell it apart: no dots.

Example words

  • سَمَك
    samak — fish
    initial position · written سمك
  • مَسْجِد
    masjid — mosque
    medial position · written مسجد

With harakat

Short vowel marks change how the letter is read. Marks are shown on س:

سَsa
سِsi
سُsu
