Short vowels: fatha, kasra, damma

Arabic spells out consonants and long vowels (ا و ي). Short vowels are normally left out and shown only by optional marks called harakat. Children’s books, the Qur’an and learner materials include them; most newspapers do not.

  • بَ
    Fatha · فتحة
    Sound: short “a”

    A small diagonal stroke ABOVE the letter. It adds a short “a” after the consonant.

    كَتَبَkataba — he wrote

  • بِ
    Kasra · كسرة
    Sound: short “i”

    A small diagonal stroke BELOW the letter. It adds a short “i” as in “sit”.

    كِتَابkitaab — book

  • بُ
    Damma · ضمة
    Sound: short “u”

    A small loop-like mark ABOVE the letter. It adds a short “u” as in “put”.

    كُتُبkutub — books
